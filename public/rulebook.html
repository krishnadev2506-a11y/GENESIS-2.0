GENESIS 2.0

Official Rulebook & Event Day Challenges

1. General Rules & Integrity

To ensure a level playing field, all teams must adhere to the following rules:

  • Repo Post-Announcement Only: All GitHub repositories must be created after the official theme announcement. No pre-built repositories are allowed.
  • Commit History Inspected: Judges will review your commit history. Ensure continuous, meaningful commits representing the team's effort over the development window.
  • No Plagiarism: Copy-pasting entire projects from the internet or other teams is strictly prohibited. Open-source libraries and frameworks are encouraged, but the core logic must be yours.
  • AI Usage Policy: AI tools (ChatGPT, Copilot, Cursor, Gemini, Claude, etc.) are permitted to assist with coding and debugging. However, you must understand all code you submit—judges will question your architecture!
  • Judges Decision is Final: All rulings made by the event judging panel are final and binding.

2. Track Expectations

Participants compete strictly within their assigned track based on their academic year.

Foundation Track (2nd & 3rd Year)

Focuses on building solid, functional applications using fundamental web/mobile architecture.

  • Expected: MVC Architecture, Database Integration, Basic Authentication, Input Validation, Clean Structure, Basic Deployment.
  • Not Expected: Complex Cloud infrastructure or heavy DevOps.

Professional Track (4th Year)

Focuses on production-grade applications, system design, and scalable infrastructure.

  • Expected: System Design, Cloud Deployment, Docker & CI/CD, AI Integration, Scalable Architecture, Monitoring & Logs.
  • Requirement: Teams must be able to justify all technical choices and architecture decisions during the defense.

3. Event Day Challenge Stations

On the main event day, teams will face technical hurdles and surprise challenges designed to test their engineering agility and teamwork.

Both Tracks

🤖 AI Engineering Battle

Teams must integrate a specific AI-driven feature into their existing application within a strict time limit (e.g., automated tagging, sentiment analysis, or a localized chatbot).

Both Tracks

⚡ Constraint Drop

A random constraint is dropped on your team mid-development. Examples: "No new NPM packages allowed for the next hour," "Offline docs only," or "Backend-only changes." You must adapt and continue building.

Both Tracks

⏱️ Lightning Feature

A surprise 45-minute feature request is given to all teams. Examples include: adding a dark mode toggle, PDF export functionality, or a quick real-time update socket connection.

Both Tracks

✨ Best UI/UX

Not a separate station, but a continuous judging metric throughout the day. Points are awarded for intuitive design, accessibility, responsiveness, and overall polish.

Professional Only

🛡️ Red Team Attack

Judges will attempt to breach your application using common vulnerabilities (SQL Injection, Broken Auth, XSS, Spam Requests, Large Uploads). Teams must patch the vulnerabilities live.

Professional Only

🗣️ Mock Technical Interview

Teams undergo a rigorous architecture defense. Judges will interrogate the team on their database design, authentication flow, application performance, scalability bottlenecks, and future improvements.
